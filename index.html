18 November 2025

Small steps beat big plans

I keep starting with a plan that is too big, then quitting in three days. What worked better: pick one small thing — twenty minutes of practice — and do it every day. The plan gets written later, after the habit sticks.

habits focus

12 November 2025

Read the error out loud

When something breaks I used to change code and hope. Now I read the error message slowly, out loud, and find the exact line it names. Nine times out of ten the answer is already written there.

4 November 2025

Write it in my own words

Copying a sentence from a lesson feels like learning, but it is only typing. If I close the page and explain the idea the way I would tell a friend, I find out at once which part I did not really understand.

learning writing

27 October 2025

Twenty-five minutes is enough

One timer, one tab, one thing. When the timer rings I stop, even if I feel like continuing. Starting again is easy when the last round ended well, and the small break keeps the work from becoming a grind.

How to add a new entry

  1. Open index.html in a text editor.
  2. Find the line that says To add a new entry.
  3. Copy one whole <article> block and paste it under the last one.
  4. Change the date, the title, the paragraph and the tags.
  5. Save the file and refresh the page in your browser.

This is the shape of one entry:

<article class="note">
  <p class="date">8 January 2026</p>
  <h3>The thing I learned</h3>
  <p class="body">What it is, in my own words.</p>
  <div class="tags">
    <span class="tag">coding</span>
  </div>
</article>
